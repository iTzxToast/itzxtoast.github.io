Servus! I’m Daniel, working at [Salesfive](https://www.salesfive.com/), where I focus on Microsoft 365, Mobile Device Management, and Post-Merger Integration in M&A projects.

I live in Munich, Germany - a beautiful city where I spend most of my free time gaming, experimenting in my homelab, and figuring things out.

My interest in IT started when I tried to host my own TeamSpeak 3 server on a Raspberry Pi. There wasn’t a native ARM version at the time, so I had to run it through an x86 emulator - that challenge got me hooked on solving complex technical problems.

These days, I run a homelab with three Proxmox nodes and a Synology NAS, where I like to experiment, automate, and occasionally break things just to fix them again.

This blog is my space to share the stuff that keeps me up at night - the problems I run into, the solutions I find, and maybe a few lessons that can save someone else the headache.

*Disclaimer:* English isn’t my native language, so I sometimes use AI tools for rephrasing or generating images. All ideas, experiences, and technical content are entirely my own.
